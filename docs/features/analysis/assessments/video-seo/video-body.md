The **Video body** assessment checks whether the text has a good length for a page where a video is the main asset. It is part of the SEO analysis of [Yoast Video SEO](https://yoast.com/product/video-seo-wordpress/).

| **Video body** | |
|---|---|
| **Name in code** | `VideoBodyAssessment` |
| **Title URL** | None |
| **Call to action URL** | [https://yoa.st/4g4](https://yoa.st/4g4) |
| **When it applies** | Always. |
| **Product** | Yoast Video SEO |
| **Content types** | Content types where Yoast Video SEO is active |
| **Related keyphrases** | ❌ |
| **Uses synonyms** | ❌ |
| **Languages** | Japanese counts characters instead of words, with different boundaries (see the scoring table). |
| **Cornerstone** | ❌ Same scoring |
| **Uses highlighting** | ❌ |
| **Uses morphology (Premium)** | ❌ |

## Scoring

| Traffic light | Score | Criterion | Feedback |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orange | 6 | 150 words or less (Japanese: 300 characters or less) | Your body copy is too short for Search Engines to understand the topic of your video, add some more content describing the contents of the video. |
| Orange | 6 | 400 words or more (Japanese: 800 characters or more) | Your body copy is quite long, make sure that the video is the most important asset on the page, read **this post** for more info. |
| Green | 9 | 151-399 words (Japanese: 301-799 characters) | Your body copy is at optimal length for your video to be recognized by Search Engines. |
